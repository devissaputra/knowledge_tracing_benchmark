# Frozen external-cohort replication protocol (2026-10-22)

**Research question.** On a previously unanalysed, different-year learner sample from ASSISTments 2015, does the pre-existing extended GRU pipeline achieve lower held-out pooled Brier loss than the pre-existing enriched PFA pipeline? The models are retrained on 2015 training learners. This tests cross-cohort *replication of the comparison*, not direct transport of the 2009 fitted model or causal effects of architecture.

- Source: official ASSISTments 2015 Skill Builder Data, `2015_100_skill_builders_main_problems.csv`, Google Drive file ID `0B_hO8cnpcIMgUGZzRnh3bHJrSjQ` linked from https://sites.google.com/site/assistmentsdata/datasets/2015-assistments-skill-builder-data.
- Require `user_id`, `log_id` (event order), `sequence_id` (100 skill-builder concepts), and binary `correct`. Require non-null, unambiguous records and unique learner-event pairs. Sort each learner chronologically using `log_id`. Never use later responses as model inputs for earlier outcomes.
- Learner sampling: uniformly sample without replacement **5,000 distinct learners**, deterministically with seed `20261022` after sorting IDs lexicographically. No filtering based on outcomes or model performance, except invalid records verified before learner sampling; log exclusions.
- Split: shuffle selected learners with seed `20261022`, then 70/15/15, disjoint between training/validation/test. A new dataset, independent of the 2009 train/validation/test learners. All model selection on training/validation; test inspected once.
- Fixed models: pre-existing stronger PFA `stronger_pfa()` (skill success/failure and cross-skill prior counts, logistic regression C=1, SAGA max_iter=1000) and `GRUKnowledgeTracer` model (32-token,16-query,64-hidden, Adam lr=0.002, validation early stopping, max 25 epochs, patience 4, random seed 42). No test-driven tuning.
- Primary estimand: interaction-weighted paired Brier-score difference `GRU - enriched PFA`. Resample **test learners** with replacement (2,000 replicates, seed 20261022), retaining all responses per selected learner. 95% percentile confidence interval. Claim directional external-cohort confirmation **only if** upper CI bound < 0 and computation/data gates pass; if the upper bound >= 0, record as not confirmed. Sign criterion is not proof of architecture causation.
- Secondary descriptive quantities: ROC-AUC, log loss, calibration/ECE, learner and interaction counts, outcome prevalence, checksums and provenance. No choosing between datasets/seeds after seeing test outcomes.
- Scope caveats: This is replication within ASSISTments in another year, on a random 5,000-learner subsample, not independent educational-system validity; 2015 skill-builder `sequence_id` concepts may not be the same units as 2009 mirror skill IDs, and enriched PFA and GRU have unequal information/capacity.
- No outcome claims before executed logs and machine-readable artifacts have been verified. Inability to retrieve/verify the official source means **blocked**, not passed.
